## ps2native-horizontal-light

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished standalone original logo for the open-source static recompiler PS2Native (ps2n), to use in its GitHub README and developer tool.
Primary request: design an elegant horizontal symbol-and-wordmark logo expressing discrete machine instructions becoming a native program. At the left, a distinctive compact geometric emblem: exactly three or four substantial square blocks on its left edge resolve into one bold continuous lowercase n-shaped form on the right. Use an economical pixel-to-solid transition with a clear coherent silhouette. The blocks must be organized with intentional spacing, not a particle cloud or loading spinner. Connect the visual concept to the project's lower-case n. The visual should feel technical, fast, contemporary and subtly rooted in the console era.
To the right of the emblem, a beautifully kerned custom lowercase wordmark "ps2n", with substantial geometric sans-serif characters. Make numeral 2 very clear. Smaller secondary wordmark "PS2Native" directly below ps2n, aligned left.
Style/medium: premium flat vector-style identity, crisp precise geometry and antialiasing, no gradients, minimal details, thoughtful optical balance.
Composition: one finished horizontal logo only, generous transparent clear space, approximately 1536 by 1024 landscape canvas.
Text (verbatim): "ps2n" and "PS2Native". No tagline or other text.
Color: emblem in rich electric blue; type in deep graphite. Flat solid fills only.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, not white and not a printed checkerboard.
Constraints: original emblem and original letterforms, no Sony or PlayStation logos, no controller shapes, no tiny fragments, no generic arrowhead, no circuitry, no badge border, no mockup, no watermark, shadows, reflections, glow, 3D, or texture. Deliver the single usable graphic, not a concept sheet.
